Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies to flyra.dz and all FLYRA services

FLYRA ("we", "us"), based in Algiers, Algeria, operates this online store. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Algerian Law No. 18-07 on the protection of natural persons in the processing of personal data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Order information: first name, last name, phone number, email (optional), wilaya, and delivery address — required to process and deliver your order.
  • Account information: email and password if you create an account (passwords are stored only as secure hashes).
  • Communication: messages you send us via WhatsApp, email, or contact forms.
  • Newsletter: your email address, only if you subscribe voluntarily.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Processing, confirming, and delivering your orders (including sharing your name/address/phone with our delivery partners).
  • Contacting you about your order status by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Sending promotional offers only if you subscribed to the newsletter — every message includes an unsubscribe option.
  • Improving our products and services through anonymous statistics.

3. What We Never Do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
  • We never share your data except with delivery partners strictly for order fulfillment, or when required by Algerian law.
  • We never store payment credentials — CCP/BaridiMob transfers are handled entirely outside our systems.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage and cookies strictly to keep your cart, language preference, and session active. No advertising trackers are used.

5. Data Retention & Security

Order records are kept for the duration required by commercial law. All data transmission is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to customer data is restricted to authorized FLYRA staff.

6. Your Rights

You may request at any time: access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your account and data, or withdrawal of newsletter consent. Contact us and we will respond within 72 hours.
