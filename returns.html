Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · 7-day returns, no questions asked

The FLYRA promise: if it doesn't fit or you don't love it, you have 7 days from delivery to return it — exchange or refund, no questions asked.

What Can Be Returned

  • Items that are unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached.
  • Items with a manufacturing defect (quality is our responsibility — we cover all return costs).
  • Wrong size — we exchange it for the right one when available.

What Cannot Be Returned

  • Items worn, washed, damaged by use, or without tags.
  • Custom-designed pieces made specifically for you (unless defective).
  • Final-sale / clearance items marked as non-returnable.

How to Start a Return

Contact us on WhatsApp

Message us within 7 days of delivery with your order number (FLYRA-XXXX) and the reason.

Get your return approval

We confirm eligibility within 24 hours and give you the return instructions.

Ship the item back

Send it from any delivery office to our Algiers address (provided on approval). Defective items: we pay the shipping.

Receive exchange or refund

Exchange ships immediately. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days via CCP/BaridiMob transfer.

Refund Details

  • COD orders → refunded via CCP/BaridiMob transfer to the account you provide.
  • CCP/BaridiMob prepaid orders → refunded to the same account.
  • Original delivery fees are refunded only when the return is due to our error (defect, wrong item).

Damaged or Wrong Item?

We're sorry — that's on us. Send us a photo of the item on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery and we'll ship a replacement right away at zero cost, or refund you fully including delivery fees.
