FAQ

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery is the easiest. You can also pay in advance via CCP transfer or BaridiMob — send us the receipt on WhatsApp and we ship immediately.

How long does delivery take?

2–5 business days anywhere in Algeria. You get a tracking code as soon as your order ships.

Is shipping really free?

Yes — on every order over 25,000 DZD, delivered anywhere in Algeria.

What if the size doesn't fit?

You have 7 days to exchange it or return it — see the Return Policy. Our AI stylist can also help you pick the right size before ordering.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Yes, as long as it hasn't shipped yet. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number as soon as possible.
